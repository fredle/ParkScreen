Your PC, on your car's screen.

ParkScreen streams a Windows monitor to your parked Tesla's browser. Video and touch go straight between your PC and the car, never through our servers.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Signed installer, updates itself. Release notes

How it works

  1. Install ParkScreen on your Windows PC. It runs from the system tray and starts with Windows if you choose.
  2. Click the ParkScreen icon in your system tray (near the clock), choose Pair a car…, and note the 6-digit code. It is valid for 5 minutes.
  3. In your car's browser, open parkscreen.web.app/car and enter the code. You only pair once.
  4. Your PC's screen appears. Touch the car's screen to tap, drag and scroll (see below).

What you get

  • Hardware-accelerated videoH.264 from your GPU (NVENC, Quick Sync or AMF), with a software fallback. Bitrate adapts to the connection.
  • Real touchTaps and drags from the car become multi-touch input on your PC, and scrolling works. Off until you allow it: start ParkScreen with --with-input (a tray switch isn't built yet).
  • Duplicate or extendDuplicate shows your main screen on the car today. Extend, where the car is a separate screen, needs a display driver that isn't released yet; the tray option stays greyed out until it is installed.
  • Private by designEach car pairs once with a short-lived code, and your PC only accepts cars it has paired with. Video and touch are peer to peer and never stored. Privacy
  • No admin rightsInstalls for your user only, updates itself in the background, and restarts only when no car is streaming.

Use only while parked. Tesla blocks video while the car is in gear, and ParkScreen does not work around that.
