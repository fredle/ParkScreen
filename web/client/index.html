Your PC, on your car's screen.

ParkScreen streams a Windows monitor to your parked Tesla's browser. Video and touch go straight between your PC and the car, never through our servers.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Signed installer, updates itself.

How it works

  1. Install ParkScreen on your Windows PC and leave it running.
  2. Click the ParkScreen icon in your system tray (near the clock), choose Pair a car…, and note the 6-digit code. It is valid for 5 minutes.
  3. In your car's browser, open parkscreen.web.app/car and enter the code. You only pair once.
  4. Your PC's screen appears. For a second screen, add a virtual display driver on the PC.

Use only while parked. Tesla blocks video while the car is in gear, and ParkScreen does not work around that. Touch control from the car is not available yet; viewing is.
